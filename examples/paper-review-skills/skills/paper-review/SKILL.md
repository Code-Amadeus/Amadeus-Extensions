# Paper review

1. Read the abstract, introduction, and conclusion first. Write each claimed
   contribution as one sentence.
2. For each claim, find the experiment, proof, or citation that supports it.
   Mark claims that have no direct support.
3. Check the method: data, baselines, ablations, statistical treatment, and
   threats to validity.
4. Separate major issues, which would change the conclusion, from minor ones
   about clarity or presentation.
5. Finish with specific questions for the authors and a short overall
   assessment.

Cite the paper's own wording when you discuss a claim, and give the section,
table, or figure number.
